Multi-task robustness under task-universal execution noise. Task-macro success is shown for MT10 and MT50 over noise levels $\lambda\in\{0,0.10,0.20,0.30,0.40\}$, where the action and observation noise standard deviations are $0.4\lambda$ and $0.025\lambda$, respectively. Each point aggregates 50 episodes per task; error bars are 95% within-task episode-bootstrap confidence intervals. The two suites use identical perturbation settings but separately trained models. These disturbed conditions are a robustness extension rather than official-clean benchmark scores.
